About Kohinoor Tier

A competitive Minecraft PvP community built around skill, fair testing, player improvement, and recognizing the best fighters.

Who We Are

Built for players. Driven by competition.

Welcome to MCPVP Tier, a community concept for Minecraft PvP players who want to test their abilities, improve their gameplay, and compete with other skilled fighters.


Our goal is to provide a clear tier ranking system, competitive leaderboards, and a place where players can showcase their skills across different PvP modes. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced player, there is always room to learn and improve.
